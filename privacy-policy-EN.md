# M2Al Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 1 November 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how the **M2Al** mobile application (the "App") handles user data.

- **Data Controller:** Ömer Azak ("we")
- **Contact:** omerazak.41@hotmail.com • Phone: +90 543 621 65 54

## Summary
M2Al respects your privacy. The analyses you create are stored **only on your device**; they are not
sent to our servers. No account is required; we do not collect your name, email, or phone number. We
do not use advertising or tracking.

## Data We Process and Why

### Data that stays only on your device
- **Analysis records:** The location text you enter/that is read, area, price, zoning/usage type, and
  the computed scores. This data is stored in a local database on your device (Apple SwiftData), is
  not synced to the cloud, and is not sent to us. You can delete records at any time.
- **Screenshots:** The listing screenshot you select is processed **on your device** (Apple Vision)
  to read its text. The image is not sent over the network and is not stored.

### Data sent to Apple services
- **Location resolution and surroundings:** The address text you enter and the coordinates derived
  from it are sent to the **Apple MapKit** service to verify the location on a map and to count
  nearby points of interest (e.g. schools, bus stops). This is subject to Apple's privacy policy. We
  do not send any information that identifies you.

## Data We Do Not Collect
- Identity data such as name, email, or phone (there is no account system).
- Advertising identifiers, location tracking, or third-party analytics/tracking data.

## Third Parties
The App uses only Apple's own services (MapKit, Vision, SwiftData). Apple's privacy
practices: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Data Retention and Deletion
Analysis records remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. You can swipe to
delete individual records, or uninstall the App to remove all of them.

## Children's Privacy
The App is suitable for ages 4+ and does not knowingly collect data from children.

## Your Rights
Depending on your country (e.g. GDPR / Turkey's KVKK), you have the right to access, correct, and
delete your data. Because your data is kept on your device, you can perform these actions directly in
the App.

## Changes
We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page.
